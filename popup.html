Tidy Watch Later

You're not on your Watch Later page. The tools only work on youtube.com/playlist?list=WL.

Tidy your YouTube Watch Later list: delete videos you have already watched, tag videos from their titles with your own AI model, and bulk-delete by tag. Open your Watch Later playlist, then pick a tool.

Describe what you want to watch. Your model scores each video on its title, channel, duration, views and age, and how much you have watched, and only the best matches stay visible on the page. Scoring starts at the top of the list.
